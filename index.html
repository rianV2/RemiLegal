RemiAccess
Privacy Terms

Private owner-operated utility

Focused Google Ads keyword research.

RemiAccess helps its owner retrieve keyword ideas, historical search metrics, forecasts, and geographic targeting information from Google Ads.

Not offered as a public service

Purpose

Narrow by design

The application is used only by its owner for market and keyword research. Its intended operations are read-oriented: discovering keyword ideas, viewing historical metrics and forecasts, and identifying Google Ads geographic targets.

RemiAccess does not create campaigns, advertisements, budgets, or automated spending. Google’s OAuth permission for Google Ads is broad, but the application limits its actual use to these research activities.

01

Private use

Only the application owner is intended to authorize and operate RemiAccess.

02

Local control

OAuth credentials and retrieved information are handled in the owner’s controlled environment.

03

No ad mutations

The application’s intended workflow does not modify campaigns, ads, bids, or budgets.

RemiAccess is an independent private utility and is not affiliated with Google.
