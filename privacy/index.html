Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Overview

RemiAccess is a private, owner-operated utility for Google Ads keyword research. It is not offered as a public service. This policy explains what information the application accesses and how that information is handled.

Information accessed

When the owner authorizes RemiAccess through Google OAuth, the application may access Google Ads information necessary to generate keyword ideas, historical search metrics, forecasts, and geographic targeting information. Google provides a broad Google Ads OAuth scope; RemiAccess limits its intended use to research-oriented operations and does not intentionally create or modify campaigns, advertisements, bids, or budgets.

How information is used

Information is used solely to perform keyword and market research requested by the owner. It is not used for advertising profiles, sold, rented, or shared with unrelated third parties.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials and retrieved research information are stored only in environments controlled by the owner. Reasonable technical measures are used to limit access. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Retention and deletion

Credentials and research information are retained only for as long as needed by the owner. They can be deleted from the owner’s systems at any time. Google account access can also be revoked through Google Account third-party connections.

Website data

This informational website does not intentionally use analytics, advertising trackers, cookies, account registration, or contact forms. GitHub Pages, which hosts this website, may process standard network and server-log information according to GitHub’s own policies.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the application’s operation or legal requirements change. The latest version will appear on this page with its revision date.
